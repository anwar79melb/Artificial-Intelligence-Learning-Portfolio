# Applied Natural Language Processing

# Course Overview

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on enabling computers to understand, process, analyze, and generate human language. It combines concepts from Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Linguistics, Machine Learning, and Data Science to transform unstructured text into meaningful knowledge and actionable insights.

Every day, enormous amounts of textual data are generated through emails, websites, social media platforms, customer reviews, chat messages, reports, scientific publications, and digital archives. Unlike structured numerical data, text is highly complex, ambiguous, context-dependent, and often noisy. NLP provides the techniques and algorithms necessary to unlock the information hidden within this data.

This course introduces the complete NLP pipeline, beginning with the fundamentals of language processing and gradually progressing toward advanced applications such as deep learning for sequence modelling, named entity recognition, question answering systems, chatbots, conversational AI, and ethical considerations in NLP.

# What Makes NLP Challenging?

Human language is remarkably difficult for machines to understand because it contains:

## Lexical Ambiguity

A single word may have multiple meanings.

Example:

```text
He went to the bank.
```

Possible meanings:

- Financial institution
- River bank


---

## Syntactic Ambiguity

A sentence may have multiple grammatical interpretations.

Example:

```text
The woman saw a girl with a telescope.
```

Who owns the telescope?

- The woman?
- The girl?

---

## Semantic Ambiguity

The intended meaning may be unclear.

Example:

```text
The truck hit the car while it was moving.
```

What was moving?

- The truck?
- The car?

---

## Language Variation

NLP systems must deal with:

- Multiple languages
- Slang
- Dialects
- Code-switching
- Abbreviations
- Misspellings
- Social media language
